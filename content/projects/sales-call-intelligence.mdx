## Outcome

**Earlier signals for Pre-Sales and deal teams.**

Sales and Pre-Sales got buyer, competitor, and deal-risk signals earlier—inside the tools they already used.

## Problem

Important details were buried in call notes and transcripts. Pre-Sales and deal teams often found buyer concerns, competitor mentions, and technical blockers too late.

## Approach

The system read call notes and transcripts, extracted buyer and competitor signals, and sent them to Slack, email, and Airtable or CRM records.
