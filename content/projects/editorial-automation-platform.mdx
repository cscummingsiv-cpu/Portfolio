## Outcome

Supported a roadmap projected to add $20M+ in annual revenue. The platform let editorial increase output without hiring at the same rate.

## Problem

Editorial throughput was capped by manual workflows. Scaling output required scaling headcount—a hard ceiling on growth and margin.

## Approach

One platform managed editorial workflows from request through publication. Shared standards and AI enrichment made it faster to add and improve automations.
