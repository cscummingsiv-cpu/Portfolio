## Outcome

Enabled nontechnical users to generate supply-performance insights in real time, eliminating 100+ hours of annual manual reporting.

## Problem

Business users depended on manual reporting to answer recurring questions about supply performance. Data expertise sat between the question and the answer.

## Approach

Multi-model system that accepts natural-language questions, generates SQL, executes securely in Snowflake, and returns tables or visualizations in real time.
